# Code of Conduct

We want AlbionPresence to be a welcoming project. Be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Maintainers may replace this short text with the full Covenant and should add a private reporting contact.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors that violate these expectations.
